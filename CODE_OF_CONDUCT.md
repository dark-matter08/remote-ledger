# Code of conduct

This project is small and the bar is simple: **be someone people want to work with.**

## What that means here

- Assume the other person is capable and acting in good faith. Most disagreements in a
  codebase are two people holding different context, not one person being careless.
- Critique the code, the design or the decision — never the person who wrote it.
- If you are told your tone landed badly, take it at face value and adjust. Intent is
  not the same as effect, and you do not get to arbitrate someone else's experience.
- Newcomers ask questions that have obvious answers. Answering them well is the whole
  job of an open project.

## What is not welcome

Harassment, insults, or sustained hostility. Sexualised language or imagery. Personal
or political attacks. Publishing anyone's private information. Deliberately derailing
discussions or wearing people down through persistence.

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere you are
identifiably representing the project.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to the maintainers at **ndelucien008@gmail.com**. You will get a
reply, the report will not be shared further than it needs to be, and you will not be
asked to justify being uncomfortable.

Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or block accounts. For anything
beyond an obvious one-off, the reasoning will be stated plainly rather than actioned
silently.

Maintainers are held to this document too, more strictly than anyone else. If one of us
is the problem, say so — to the address above, or publicly if that feels safer.
